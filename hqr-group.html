Holonomic Quantum Research Group (HQRG)

About HQRG

The Holonomic Quantum Research Group (HQRG) is dedicated to advancing the study of quantum information, spacetime emergence, and fundamental physics.

Our Mission

HQRG explores the deep connections between quantum mechanics, gravity, and information theory. We aim to develop a unified framework—Holonomic Quantum Reality (HQR)—that explains the emergent nature of spacetime through quantum information.

Research Areas

  • 🔹 Quantum Information & Spacetime
  • 🔹 Holography & Higher-Dimensional Physics
  • 🔹 Black Hole Entropy & Gravitational Wave Studies
  • 🔹 AI-Driven Quantum Physics Research
  • 🔹 Experimental Tests of Informational Gravity
