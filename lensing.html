This visualization demonstrates the differences in gravitational lensing predictions between standard General Relativity and Holonomic Quantum Reality (HQR). As light passes near a massive object, its path bends due to the curvature of spacetime.

HQR predicts additional bending due to quantum informational structures in higher dimensions that affect spacetime geometry. This leads to a measurable difference in lensing angle that could potentially be detected with next-generation gravitational wave detectors and astronomical observations.

Use the slider to adjust the mass of the gravitational lens and observe how the light paths differ between the two theories. Notice that HQR consistently predicts slightly stronger lensing effects, which become more pronounced with increasing mass.
